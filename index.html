Questions, answered

Quick answers before you join.

Are the mock tests timed?

Yes. Each mock follows the real exam pattern, with sectional timers and negative marking.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The whole site works on any screen size.

Which exams does MockZone cover?

We cover SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC GD Constable, RRB NTPC, RRB Group D, IBPS PO, SBI PO, SBI Clerk and MP Police Constable. Each exam has its own page with mocks made for that exam, so you only practice what you are preparing for.

Is MockZone free to use?

Yes, you can start for free. Creating an account costs nothing, and every exam has a free mock test you can attempt to see how our tests feel before you decide on anything else.

What is the difference between free and paid mocks?

The free mock is a sample test that shows you the exam pattern, the timer and how results look. The paid series gives you the full set of mock tests for an exam, with detailed analytics and solutions for every question, so you can find weak topics and fix them before exam day.

How close are the mocks to the real exam?

Our mocks are built around the pattern of each exam, with timed sections, the same question style and negative marking where the real exam has it. The goal is that the real paper feels familiar, so you can spend your energy on solving instead of getting used to the format.

How do I get free notes and daily updates?

Join our Telegram community. We share free notes, mock test alerts and exam updates there. Join our Telegram and you will not miss a new test.

Do you have previous year question papers (PYQs)?

Yes. Real questions from past exam papers are added alongside our mocks, organised by exam and year, so you can see exactly what has been asked before.
